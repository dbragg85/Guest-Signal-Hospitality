# This Week in Hospitality Signals: Review response speed that protects guest trust

Search trends, guest behavior signals, and operator takeaways for restaurants.

## Opening Signal
Prospective guests treat unanswered reviews as **unresolved operations**. Speed signals that someone is accountable—even when the reply is brief.

## What People Are Searching
- **Google review monitoring** — Operators look for systems that surface negative reviews before they age.
- **How to respond to restaurant reviews** — Template demand rises when ratings dip.

## Operator Deep Dive: Response SLAs That Actually Ship
| Severity | Target response | Owner |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| 1–2 star, specific miss | ≤24 hours | Manager |
| 3 star, mixed | ≤48 hours | Owner or lead |
| 4–5 star | ≤72 hours | Shared pool |

Use three template spines—recovery, clarification, gratitude—and customize only the middle sentence. Guests spot copy-paste when the opening and closing are generic but the middle references their table, daypart, or dish.

## Guest Signal Takeaway
Speed without specificity feels automated. Specificity without speed feels indifferent. You need both.

## Action Checklist for Operators
- Turn on alerts for new 1–3 star reviews.
- Draft three response spines this week.
- Assign a single owner per daypart for review duty.
- Measure median hours-to-response monthly.
- Cross-check responses against in-store recovery notes.
